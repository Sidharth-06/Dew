## Overview  

**Dew is an open-source music experience that reimagines how we listen, share, and connect.**  
It’s more than a player — Dew adapts to your surroundings, lets you share music playfully, and brings friends together in perfect sync.  

This repository is **read-only** and serves as a showcase of the project’s design and features.  
You are free to fork, explore, and remix.  

---

## Features  

### 🎧 Always With You  
Music that remembers. Dew resumes right where you left off, making every return seamless.  

### 🃏 Share the Vibe  
Sharing music should be as playful as the music itself.  
With Dew, you can flick a song like a card across the room and watch a friend catch it instantly.  

### 🎼 Together, In Sync  
Friends can join your beat — with jam sessions that sync playback and timing perfectly across devices.  
Whether side by side or worlds apart, you’ll always hear the same moment.  

### 📂 Endless Discovery  
Playlists, trending tracks, hidden gems — Dew keeps your soundtrack evolving, always ready with what’s next.  

---

## 🧠 Intelligent Features  

### 🔉 Adaptive Flow  
Your music reacts to your environment in real time.  
- **When someone speaks** → Dew lowers the volume so voices are clear.  
- **When the room grows quiet** → the music rises back gently.  
- **In noisy places** → playback balances itself so it never feels overwhelming.  

It feels like the music *understands the moment*.  

---

### 🎙️ Awareness of Sound  
Dew can sense the difference between:  
- Everyday conversations 🗣️  
- Busy traffic 🚗  
- Natural surroundings 🌿  
- Complete silence 🌌  

Each setting shapes how your playback feels — softer in chatter, vibrant in open spaces, expansive in silence.  

---

### 📜 Smart Lyrics Sync  
Lyrics don’t just scroll — they breathe with the song.  
- Lines appear in sync with the beat.  
- Timing adjusts automatically when exact matches aren’t available.  
- Every track feels like it has its own personal karaoke mode.  

---

### 🔒 On-Device Intelligence  
All of this happens **directly on your device**:  
- No audio ever leaves your phone.  
- No servers process your surroundings.  
- Everything is private, instant, and secure.  

Your music adapts to your world — but never leaves your hands.
